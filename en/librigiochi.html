Branching adventures

Gamebooks

by Peter Palmer

Stories where you make the choices, and every mistake has a price. From the Underworld of the Odyssey to the Ragnarök of the Nine Worlds, all the way to the pyramids of Egypt.

An adventure that gets teenagers off their phones and, one choice at a time, introduces them to the Greek, Norse and Egyptian myths.

❧

Greek mythology · ages 14+

The Oar on Your Shoulder

A Gamebook of Greek Mythology

A storm snaps the ship's mast. Anfialo, grandson of Odysseus, wakes on a grey beach under a sky he does not know, with a young tawny dog as his only guide.

In the realm of shadows everyone wants to know his name. It is up to you to choose whom to trust, when to fight and when to flee, to settle the debt his family opened with the sea.

Norse mythology · ages 14+

Even the Iron Wept

A Gamebook of Norse Mythology

Ketil is sixteen, with the strength of a giant and a past nobody will tell him about. He is an apprentice in a village forge, until a one-eyed old man asks him his mother's name.

Then a boy swears to avenge a dead god on him. To defend yourself you will travel among gods, giants and the dead in search of witnesses: it is the hardest trial in the Nine Worlds.

Egyptian mythology · ages 10+

The Ink in the Sand

A Gamebook of Egyptian Mythology

You are sixteen, with a scribe's palette and a short bronze blade. One morning in the market of Memphis, a barefoot girl snaps the slave traders' rope and leaps onto a roof where only you knew how to jump. You run after her, and from that moment the king's decree is hunting you too.

To follow her south you will have to read what nobody taught you: spells carved inside the pyramids, signs on temple walls, a scroll that was never meant for your eyes. In Egypt, a written name holds the one who bears it.
