Legal notice and privacy

Last updated: 26 October 2026

Who runs this site

This site is run by Pierpalmiro Stroppa, Switzerland, under the Intrecci di Carta label. For any question: info@intreccidicarta.com.

What data the site collects

The site uses no cookies, has no forms to fill in, does not measure visits and shows no ads.

Fonts and images are hosted on the site itself: no data goes to Google Fonts or to other external services.

Your browser remembers the language you chose in an internal storage area of your device (localStorage). This data is never sent to anyone, and you can delete it in your browser settings.

Where the site is hosted

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, a service of GitHub Inc. (United States). Like every server, GitHub logs the IP address of visitors for technical and security reasons, under its own privacy statement: GitHub Privacy Statement.

If you write to us

If you send us an email, we use your address and your message only to reply to you. We never share them with anyone.

Links to other sites

Links to Google Play and to other sites take you away from here: the rules and privacy policies of those sites apply there.

Your rights

Under the Swiss Federal Act on Data Protection (FADP) and, if you live in the European Union, the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), you can ask what data we hold about you, have it corrected or have it deleted. Write to info@intreccidicarta.com.
