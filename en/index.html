Her mother discovered something. That is why she died.

Thriller

Scritto nel fuoco

Three missing. No bodies. No trace.

On 3 July 1944 two thousand men climbed up to Cevo by four roads and burned the village house by house. Eighty years later Chiara Belotti, whom her family calls Dina, walks back up that road in the procession, as she does every year, for a great-grandfather who died in that fire.

She is thirty-five, an inspector in Como, and a diagnosis has just sent her home: without her gun, without her driving licence, without the case she was working on her own. Men from a certain crowd who vanish leaving nothing behind, and who, as far as everyone is concerned, simply went off of their own accord.

Dina wants to get back on the case, and to get back on it she has to turn up clean for her medical in February. So she investigates in secret, with a colleague signing in her place, while around the lake they start finding bodies with too many stab wounds for a brawl. The papers come up with a name: the Angel of Death. The prosecutor's office wants a single culprit, and soon a face.

For Dina, it doesn't add up. And she can't tell anyone.

The mystery series

The Investigations of Maresciallo Iaccarino

Four mysteries among villages, vineyards and provincial silences. Maresciallo Pietro Iaccarino has a dog, Diego, a method as slow as a restoration, and the bad habit of listening.

Vol. 1

L'Arazzo dei Morti

Croceverde, Umbria, October

Some restorations tell a different story when you look at them from the back.

The story

Maresciallo Pietro Iaccarino is on holiday, visiting an old friend, with his dog Diego and no wish for trouble. Then, in the village's restoration workshop, an ancient tapestry comes back to light after a century, and with it a death no one can explain.

Iaccarino has no jurisdiction, no colleagues, no hurry: all he has is his method, slow as a restoration, thread by thread. But in a village where everyone has always known everyone, what could be more dangerous than someone who listens?

Vol. 2

La Resina dell'Inverno

Pieve Castelvecchio, Garfagnana, late November

Nothing ever happened in Pieve Castelvecchio. The only true stories the village had were the accidents in the mountains.

The story

The stone village on the ridge is getting ready for the Sant'Andrea fair: even a famous first violin is coming for the concert. Then, in the days of the festival, a death everyone is in a hurry to call an accident.

This time Iaccarino is not on holiday: he is at home, in the valley he has commanded for years, among people who greet him by name. He has the authority, but not the distance. And in a village he knows by heart, where does a lie hide?

Vol. 3

L'Oleandro delle Nozze

Roccafonda, Alto Lazio, August

As we say around here: some truths don't fall. They're pushed.

The story

At Villa Sàvelli the wedding of the year is being celebrated: the monumental staircase, the oleander in bloom, the whole village in its Sunday best. Iaccarino is there among the guests, in plain clothes, with no jurisdiction and no wish for trouble. Then, in front of everyone, a man falls down the forty steps, and the party ends right there.

An accident, they say: a misstep, the heat, his age. But Iaccarino has seen a detail that doesn't fit. And in a village where weddings are prepared for months, even the secrets dress well.

about 45,000 words

Vol. 4

Il Gallo Nero Ubriaco

Montecroce in Chianti, September, grape harvest

Wine always tells you where it comes from. The trouble is the people who don't want to hear it.

The story

At Podere Fontemòrta a crate of prized grapes vanishes in the night: in its place, rotten bunches. The next morning the caretaker is dead in the cellar, and Bacco, the black rooster on the label, is blind drunk in the farmyard. An accident, everyone says.

Iaccarino is there convalescing, the guest of an old friend, with no uniform and no authority: sparkling water, his dog Diego and the bad habit of watching. And three questions: who brought the rot in where everything was sound? Where is the good crate? And the rooster, why was he drunk?
