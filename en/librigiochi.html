Branching adventures

Gamebooks

by Peter Palmer

Stories where you make the choices, and every mistake has a price. From the Underworld of the Odyssey to the Ragnarök of the Nine Worlds, all the way to the pyramids of Egypt.

An adventure that gets teenagers off their phones and, one choice at a time, introduces them to the Greek, Norse and Egyptian myths.

❧

Greek mythology · ages 10+

The Oar on Your Shoulder

A Gamebook of Greek Mythology

The night the ship's mast snaps, you grip your oar and wait for the wave. You wake on a grey beach, under a sky you do not know. Beside you there is only a young tawny dog that seems to know where to go. And sooner or later, everyone you meet wants to know your name.

Your name is Anfialo. You are the son of Eurylochus and the grandson of Odysseus, and your family has an open debt with the sea. To settle it you will have to choose at every step which road to take, whom to trust, when to fight and when to run, and what to answer those who put you to the test.

Only one road leads all the way to the end.

Norse mythology · ages 10+

Even the Iron Wept

A Gamebook of Norse Mythology

You have seen sixteen winters and you are an apprentice in a village forge, where the world of men ends with the last ploughed field. They call you the troll of the forge, because you lift anvils that two men cannot shift. The smith who raised you found you in a basket beside the tempering cauldron, with an iron ring around your neck, too wide for a human finger.

Then, one snowy night, a one-eyed old man brings you an eight-legged grey horse to shoe, and before he leaves he asks you your mother's name. A few days later a boy arrives who has sworn to avenge a dead god on you. He cannot be beaten. He can be asked for a judgement.

You will climb among the gods, cross the lands of the giants or go down where the dead dwell, looking for witnesses in the hardest trial of the Nine Worlds.

You are Ketil. Remember your name.

Egyptian mythology · ages 10+

The Ink in the Sand

A Gamebook of Egyptian Mythology

You are sixteen, with a scribe's palette and a short bronze blade. One morning in the market of Memphis, a barefoot girl snaps the slave traders' rope and leaps onto a roof where only you knew how to jump. You run after her, and from that moment the king's decree is hunting you too.

To follow her south you will have to read what nobody taught you: spells carved inside the pyramids, signs on temple walls, a scroll that was never meant for your eyes. In Egypt, a written name holds the one who bears it.

You are Hori. Learn what may be written. And what may not.
